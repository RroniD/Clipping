AUTOCLIPPER - HOW TO USE (no tech skills needed)
================================================

WHAT IT DOES
  It watches the streamers on your list, finds the moments worth clipping, cuts them into
  vertical TikTok/Shorts/Reels videos with captions, and puts them in your REVIEW QUEUE.
  Nothing is ever posted by itself: you approve each clip and post it yourself.


EVERY DAY
  1. Double-click "AutoClipper" on your desktop.
     Two black windows open (keep them open) and the review queue opens in your browser.
  2. On your phone (same Wi-Fi): open the review queue. Easiest: on the laptop's queue page tap
     "Open on your phone" and scan the QR code with your phone camera.
  3. When a clip is ready your phone buzzes (ntfy app, see SETUP). Open the queue and:
       - watch the clip
       - if it has a yellow warning box, watch it to the end: it may be a spoiler -> reject it
       - tap a different hook or title if you like one better, then "Re-render"
       - wrong picture on top? change "Layout", then "Re-render"
       - press APPROVE (or REJECT)
  4. After approving: a green timer shows how long you have to post. Tap "Download", tap
     "Copy caption", post it on TikTok (and YouTube / Instagram if the campaign pays for them),
     then paste the post link into "Posted it?" so you can track it.
  5. To stop: close the two black windows.


A STREAM YOU MISSED (VOD)
  Option A: on the queue page tap "Make clips from a VOD", paste the Twitch link, tap
            "Make clips". The clips appear in the queue by themselves (20-60 minutes).
  Option B: double-click "Process a VOD.bat" in the Clipping folder and paste the link.


LAUNCH DAY (GTA 6, November 19)
  - Make sure the streamers you clip are on your list (see SETUP, step 4).
  - Start AutoClipper BEFORE they go live and leave the laptop plugged in, with sleep turned off
    (Settings > System > Power > Screen and sleep > Never, while plugged in).
  - Clips arrive 1-3 minutes after each moment. Post the good ones FAST: early clips win.
  - Spoiler rule: anything with a yellow warning (cutscene, story mission, "spoilers" in chat)
    -> reject unless you are 100% sure it's free-roam gameplay. Rockstar takes those down.


SETUP (once)
  1. Double-click "Check setup.bat". It tells you, in plain words, what's missing and how to fix it.
  2. Phone notifications: install the "ntfy" app, tap +, and subscribe to the topic that
     "Check setup" (or: python -m clipper.notify --setup) shows you.
  3. Phone can't open the queue? Your home Wi-Fi must be "Private": Settings > Network &
     internet > Wi-Fi > your network > Network profile type > Private. When Windows asks about
     Python and the firewall, click Allow.
  4. Streamers you're allowed to clip go in streamers.toml (or ask Claude to add them).
     Campaign rules (tags, disclosure, length) go in campaigns.toml (or ask Claude).


WHEN SOMETHING BREAKS
  - First: double-click "Check setup.bat" and do what it says.
  - Then: double-click "Update AutoClipper.bat" (gets the newest version) and try again.
  - A black window shows red text / closed by itself: take a photo of the last lines and send it
    to Claude.
  - Clips feel wrong (cut too early/late, wrong layout, bad hook): tell Claude which clip and
    what's wrong. Your approve/reject decisions also teach the tool over time.
  - The laptop went to sleep or the internet dropped: live mode reconnects by itself. If no clips
    come for a long time while the streamer is live, close both windows and start again.
